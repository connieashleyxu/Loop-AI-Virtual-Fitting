Production Engineering & Computer Vision

On-Device 3D Computer Vision & Mesh Fitting Pipeline

Loop's technical architecture translates a private 60-second 3D mobile scan into precise body landmark coordinates—matching shoppers to multi-brand sizing grids with 92% fit accuracy compared to master tailors.

🎯 92% Fit Accuracy
Validated directly against professional master tailor physical tape measurements.
📐 0.3" MAE Variance
Mean Absolute Error across key anatomical landmarks to eliminate mesh distortion.
🔒 Bank-Level Privacy
Zero photo or video storage on servers; camera streams convert instantly to anonymous 3D mesh.
  • Silhouette & Pose Landmark Detection: Leverages advanced computer vision models to map human silhouettes and extract 3D body keypoints in real time.
  • Parametric 3D Mesh Reconstruction: Fits landmark coordinate vectors to a 3D parametric shape model, simulating garment fit and continuous body curvature.
  • Volumetric Measurement Extraction: Calculates continuous underbust contours, chest volume, and band/cup geometry without manual tape measures.
  • Encrypted Mathematical Mesh Transmission: Converts local camera buffers into anonymous, encrypted 3D shape coordinates—guaranteeing 100% user privacy.
