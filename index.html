Privacy-By-Design Core Invariants

User privacy is guaranteed at the architectural level. Raw imagery is never stored, logged, or uploaded.

  • Zero Photo/Video Storage: Raw RGB camera frames exist strictly in volatile RAM buffers during keypoint extraction and are immediately overwritten.
  • Client-Side Feature Reduction: Video streams are converted on-device into anonymous 3D mathematical keypoint coordinates.
  • Encrypted Vector Payload: Cloud transmission is restricted strictly to anonymous shape parameters and contour measurements—never visual imagery.
  • Stateless Garment Queries: Size chart recommendations are evaluated via stateless requests without storing linkable biometric PII.
